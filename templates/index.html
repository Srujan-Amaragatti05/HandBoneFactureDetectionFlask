Bone fractures, commonly referred to as broken bones, occur when the force applied to a bone exceeds its strength, resulting in a disruption of its structure. They can stem from various causes such as accidents, falls, sports injuries, or underlying medical conditions. Fractures manifest through symptoms like pain, swelling, bruising, and loss of function in the affected area. Diagnosis typically involves physical examination and imaging tests like X-rays. Treatment aims to realign the bone fragments and promote healing, ranging from immobilization with casts to surgical intervention for complex fractures. Complications may arise, including delayed healing or infection. Prompt and appropriate management is vital for ensuring optimal recovery and minimizing long-term issues associated with bone fractures.
